Software & Projects

Numerical optimization, parallel solvers, and sensitivity analysis in scientific software.

GPU optimization in PETSc/TAO

I develop dense BFGS implementations in PETSc/TAO. Single-node performance evaluations compare recursive, compact dense, and intermediate dense formulations on CPUs and GPUs on Polaris and Frontier.

Additional work includes ADMM, variable-metric three-operator splitting, and proximal optimization solvers.

  • BFGS preprint
  • BFGS slides (PDF)
  • ADMM tutorial (PDF)
  • PETSc source repository

TaoTerm: composable optimization objectives

I developed TaoTerm objects for composing and modifying optimization objectives in PETSc/TAO. Part of the PIONEER thrust on computationally efficient composable solvers.

  • CASS BoF 2026 slides (PDF)
  • GitLab

Adjoint sensitivity analysis in PFLOTRAN

I develop adjoint-based sensitivity analysis in PFLOTRAN for subsurface reactive transport and physics-constrained machine-learning methods for wetland systems.

Terrestrial Wetland Function and Resilience Science Focus Area. Summer 2025–present.
