Hansol Suh

Predoctoral Appointee, Argonne National Laboratory

I develop numerical optimization algorithms and scientific software for high-performance computing at Argonne National Laboratory. My work in PETSc/TAO includes GPU implementations of quasi-Newton methods, proximal optimization solvers, and composable optimization objectives.

I earned an MSc in Computational Science and Engineering at Georgia Tech, where I worked with Tobin Isaac on numerical optimization and parallel computing.

I am seeking opportunities in scientific computing, numerical optimization, and research software engineering.
